## Direction contract

THESIS: A Nordic daily's fact-box page: every house is a headline plus a ruled strip of facts, and the page refuses the photo-card grid with badges that every listing site ships.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, near-black ink, one cobalt signal for what is new and for links; hairline rules do all the structure; one face, Schibsted Grotesk, in three sizes and tabular numerals; state is a typographic mark (weight, rule, strike), never a coloured pill.
STORY: The reader lands on the edition line and the count of new houses, reads the new ones as headlines with price, bedrooms and locality in a ruled strip, then scans the current catalogue as a compact register, and trusts the footer's source ledger that nothing was missed.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead as a single hairline-ruled line (name left, edition date centre, counts right). Below, "Novedades" as a section rule with the day label, then the first new house as a full-width entry: headline at 2 steps up, fact strip (precio · dorm · localidad · fuente) in tabular figures, a small squared photo on the right column at 1/3 width. No hero, no cards.
FORM: Swedish public-sector identity programme's fact box (faktaruta) fused with the Nordic daily front page; candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key 5b3b3295. Raises: from the timetable rack, rank by weight and case at one body size; from Japanese high-density web, hairline module discipline with no wasted margin inside a module; from Ikeda, total commitment to black on white with grey reserved for de-emphasis only.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
